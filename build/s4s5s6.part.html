Metrics & rollout

One booking is worth a year of storage margin.

Storage is a great wedge and a thin margin. Rental on the same asset, on the same lot, is the same relationship at ~12× the take per transaction — and it makes the storage side stickier, not weaker.

Phase 1 · Q4 '26

Phoenix pilot

One RV-dense metro. ~200 stored Class B/C vehicles, concierge onboarding, manual vehicle vetting, Neighbor staff shadowing the first 50 handoffs.

Gate: ≥5% activation · <1% disputes
Phase 2 · H1 '27

RVshare cohort

Make the partnership bidirectional: RVshare-referred vehicles arrive rental-eligible, and "store it free" co-marketing turns their owner base into instant supply.

Gate: host opt-in ≥30% in pilot metros
Phase 3 · H2 '27

GA + boats + depot hubs

Boats as the long tail. Commercial-lot operators — the Fifth Wall / Acadia / Jamestown side of the network — become staffed depot hubs with fleet tooling.

Gate: unit economics ≥ pilot at 10× volume
Risks

What could kill it, and what we'd do about it.

RiskWhy it's realMitigation
Insurance coverageA stored vehicle and a rented vehicle are different underwriting problems; the $1M host protection doesn't cover a renter driving away.Bolt-on rental coverage via a specialty carrier, priced into the renter's service fee — the RVshare/Outdoorsy model. Rental stays off until coverage binds.
Host disruption & churnStrangers on the lot is exactly what some storage hosts signed up to avoid. Silent resentment becomes supply churn.Consent-first (screen 6), per-request veto, frequency caps, and real pay — the Handoff Host tier. Watch the opt-out guardrail weekly.
RVshare relationshipNeighbor's rental side competes with the partner that currently sends it storage demand.Frame as bidirectional flywheel and bring them into Phase 2 economics before GA. The renegotiation window is a date on the calendar, not a surprise.
First-time-lister qualityStored vehicles skew idle; idle vehicles skew unmaintained. A bad first trip burns the renter and the lot.Eligibility gates (model year, days since last movement, photo review), a pre-rental inspection add-on Handoff Hosts can perform — for a fee.

Assumptions to validate in week one

Five things this design assumes that I'd pressure-test with internal data before writing a line of code: (1) current storage protection is bolt-on-extendable for rental rather than needing a new carrier; (2) identity verification depth today (ID scan on file?) — determines whether rental eligibility is one click or one flow; (3) whether storage hosts can already see what's parked on their lot in real time — shapes the consent UX; (4) the 20% take-rate model against Neighbor's actual cost per handoff; (5) whether commercial-lot operators want fleet tooling now or in Phase 3.
