Neighbor already refers away the bigger half of the transaction.

Neighbor and RVshare have a live partnership — and it runs in exactly one direction. RVshare sends its owners to Neighbor to find storage. Neighbor sends nobody back, and captures none of the rental economics on a vehicle parked on its own network.

The brief: help renters who store RVs and boats on Neighbor also rent those vehicles out — wireframe the touchpoints across the journey that maximize adoption, and prototype at least one of them. The prototype in section 3 is fully clickable.
